# Changelog

`1.0.1`
* Fixed Grasp of the Undying's bonus max health being taken away whenever your stats updated at full health (for example, healing to full could drop you from 300 to 200).
* Electrocute now has a 4 second cooldown after each lightning strike. Hits during the cooldown don't build stacks. Adjustable with the new Electrocute > Cooldown config setting (0 turns it off).
* Now requires R2API_RecalculateStats.

`1.0.0`
* Split out of SettMod 4.6.3. Works on every survivor, adds a No Keystone option, config for every number, and a public API.
